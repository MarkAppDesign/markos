MarkOS

A personal life dashboard, built for and used by one person.

MarkOS brings together the things its owner tracks day to day: tasks, notes, habits, projects, people and their calendar. It runs on a private server that only the owner's own devices can reach. It is not a public service, and there is no sign-up.

With the owner's permission, MarkOS reads their Google Calendar so their events show on their dashboard, and keeps two calendars of its own in that account: one for events added from the dashboard and one for birthdays.
